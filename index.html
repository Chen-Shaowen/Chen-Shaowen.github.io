Interpretable by design 以可解释性为核心

Making speech generation
controllable and physically grounded. 让语音生成更可控，
也更贴近物理机理。

I am a researcher at Nagoya University working at the intersection of interpretable speech generation, structured acoustic modeling, neural source-filter systems, and time-frequency signal analysis.

My work exposes frequency, amplitude, phase, excitation, and resonance instead of hiding them inside a black box—building high-quality speech models that remain measurable, editable, and efficient.

我现为名古屋大学研究员，研究方向涵盖可解释语音生成、结构化声学建模、神经声源—滤波器系统与时频信号分析。

我的研究显式建模频率、幅值、相位、声源激励与共振特性，使高质量语音模型不再只是黑箱，而是可测量、可编辑且高效的信号处理系统。

01 / Research

Research directions研究方向

A signal-first research program connecting interpretable models, neural generation, and real-world acoustic sensing.

以信号机理为起点，连接可解释建模、神经生成与真实环境中的声学感知。

A

Interpretable speech generation可解释语音生成

Neural vocoders that preserve explicit pitch, phase, excitation, and resonance for high-quality synthesis and flexible modification.

在高质量生成与灵活变换中显式保留基频、相位、声源激励和共振特性的神经声码器。

QHM-GAN · QHARMA-GAN
B

Structured acoustic modeling结构化声学建模

High-order sinusoidal and quasi-harmonic models for precise, sequence-wise estimation of frequency, amplitude, and phase.

利用高阶正弦与准谐波模型，对频率、幅值和相位进行高精度、序列级参数估计。

High-order SM · Structured frequency
C

Time-frequency signal analysis时频信号分析

Concentrated, noise-robust representations for fast-varying signals, with applications in aero-engine vibration monitoring.

面向快变信号构建高聚集、抗噪的时频表示，并应用于航空发动机振动监测。

Stat-SST · IF estimation
D

Robust acoustic perception鲁棒声学感知

Harmonic and time-frequency features for anomaly detection and interpretable listening in complex acoustic environments.

利用谐波及时频特征开展异常声音检测，探索复杂环境中的可解释听觉感知。

Anomaly sound · Diagnostics
